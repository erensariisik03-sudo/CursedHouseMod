V1 changes:
- Removed all TouchScreenKeyboard constructor/Open/characterLimit hooks.
- Intercepts TMP_InputField.ActivateInputFieldInternal and legacy InputField.ActivateInputFieldInternal.
- Does NOT call the original activation method, preventing Unity from creating its own keyboard path.
- Creates an invisible 1x1 Android EditText directly through JNI from the loaded .so.
- No InputFilter / maxLength is applied to the native EditText.
- Native text is polled and pushed into Unity from OnUpdateSelected.
- Closes the native keyboard through DeactivateInputField, OnDeselect, and OnSubmit hooks.
- Supports both TMP_InputField and legacy InputField using the supplied dump RVAs.
